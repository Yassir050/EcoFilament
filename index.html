Turning waste into a resource

My proposal is to collect suitable plastic bottles, recycle their material, and transform it into filament for 3D printers. A 3D printer uses a special plastic filament to create objects layer by layer.

اقتراحي هو جمع القنينات البلاستيكية المناسبة وإعادة تدوير مادتها وتحويلها إلى خيوط للطابعات ثلاثية الأبعاد. تستعمل الطابعة ثلاثية الأبعاد خيطاً بلاستيكياً خاصاً لصناعة الأشياء طبقة بعد طبقة.

Plastic bottles→Recycling→Filament→3D objects

07 / 7:15–8:35

Challenges & solutions

01 · Different plastics

Not every plastic is suitable.

Solution: Identify, separate, and test materials first.

02 · Filament quality

Uneven diameter or poor quality may cause printing problems.

Solution: Apply quality control and test the final product.

03 · Equipment and costs

Recycling equipment and energy can be expensive.

Solution: Cooperate with schools, universities, and recycling centers.

04 · Safety

Plastic processing needs suitable equipment and controlled conditions.

Solution: Trained people should work in appropriate facilities.
